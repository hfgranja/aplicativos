PEC Observation is the official app for Curriculum Specialist Teachers (PECs) in the São Paulo state school system to conduct structured classroom observations with AI support.

KEY FEATURES

• School and teacher registration
• Lesson observation planning (subject, grade, theme)
• Offline audio recording of classroom sessions
• Automatic audio transcription using AI (Whisper)
• AI-generated pedagogical feedback based on SEDUC-SP rubric
• Human review and approval workflow
• Action plan creation and PDF export
• Full audit trail for LGPD compliance
• Automatic background sync when connected

WORKS OFFLINE
All field operations — observations, planning, and recording — work without internet. Sync happens automatically in the background.

PRIVACY & LGPD
Audio files are automatically deleted after 7 days. No student-identifiable data is stored. Compliant with Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD).

WHO IS IT FOR?
Exclusive to credentialed PECs in the São Paulo state school system. Requires an account provided by the São Paulo State Education Secretariat (SEDUC-SP).
